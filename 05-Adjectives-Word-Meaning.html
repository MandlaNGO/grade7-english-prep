Module 5: Adjectives, Comparatives & Word Meaning

Language & Grammar - adjectives; comparative and superlative forms; synonyms and antonyms; prefixes, suffixes and roots.

Grade 7 · English Home Language (HL) · Term 4 exam revision · 2 x 60 minutes

By the end of this module you can:

  • Identify and use adjectives, including the correct order of adjectives.
  • Form comparative and superlative adjectives correctly.
  • Use synonyms and antonyms to improve writing.
  • Break words into prefixes, roots and suffixes and work out meanings.

1. Learner Notes

Adjectives

  • An adjective describes a noun or pronoun: a brave girl, the tall tree.
  • Attributive adjectives come before the noun (a red car); predicative adjectives come after a linking verb (the car is red).
  • Order of adjectives: opinion, size, age, shape, colour, origin, material, purpose (a lovely little old round brown Italian leather riding boot).

Comparative and superlative forms

AdjectiveComparative (2)Superlative (3+)
talltallertallest
happyhappierhappiest
bigbiggerbiggest
beautifulmore beautifulmost beautiful
goodbetterbest
badworseworst
farfurtherfurthest
  • Short adjectives (1 syllable) add -er / -est.
  • Adjectives ending in -y change y to i (happy - happier).
  • Long adjectives (2+ syllables) use more / most.
  • Some are irregular (good - better - best).

Use synonyms to avoid repeating words and antonyms to create contrast in writing.

7. Remember this!

One-glance recap - if a line feels fuzzy, scroll back to the notes.

  • Comparative compares 2 things (taller); superlative compares 3 or more (tallest).
  • Learn the irregulars: good - better - best; bad - worse - worst.
  • Synonym = same meaning. Antonym = opposite meaning.
  • Prefix before the root (un-, dis-, re-), suffix after it (-ful, -less) - spot how the meaning changes.

Lesson Plan

Learning objectives

  • Identify and use adjectives, including the correct order of adjectives.
  • Form comparative and superlative adjectives correctly.
  • Use synonyms and antonyms to improve writing.
  • Break words into prefixes, roots and suffixes and work out meanings.

Prior knowledge

Learners know that adjectives describe nouns.

Resources

Grammar chart, worksheet, dictionary, word cards.

Teaching steps

TimeActivity
10 minRevise adjectives; teach the order of adjectives.
15 minTeach comparative and superlative rules with a table; practise.
10 minSynonyms and antonyms: matching and 'improve the sentence' task.
20 minPrefixes, roots and suffixes: build and decode words.
5 minExit ticket.

Differentiation, Exit Ticket & Homework

Support

  • Provide an adjective-order card strip and a prefix/suffix reference sheet.

Extension

  • Write a paragraph describing a place that uses at least 6 adjectives in the correct order.

Exit ticket

Give the comparative and superlative of 'far'.

Homework

Use the dictionary to find the root of 5 words and write their meanings.

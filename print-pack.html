DayFocusTasks (45-60 min)Done
Day 1BaselineMini-mock: Trainer, All categories, 20 questions. Start the error log.
Day 2ComprehensionModule 1 worksheet + exam question. 1 newspaper article with 4 questions.
Day 3SummaryModule 9 worksheet ('Be Safe Online') + exam passage. Count your words every time.
Day 4PoetryModule 7: annotate 'Rain at Night', then Module 3 figures quiz.

Day 6Language AModules 4 + 5: nouns, pronouns, articles, adjectives, word meaning drills.
Day 7Language BModule 6: sentence types, punctuation, verbs, direct/indirect speech.
Day 8Light dayTrainer flashcards only (20 min) + read your error log out loud.
Day 9Full mockMock Paper 2 in 90 minutes, no phone. Mark it with the memo.
Day 10Fix the gapsRework every mock mistake in the error log. Redo those Trainer categories.
Day 11TransactionalModule 11: memorise formats; write 2 short texts timed (30 min).
Day 12Essay + techniqueModule 10: planning grid + intro + 1 TEE paragraph. Module 12 exam technique.
Day 13Oral dayRehearse the oral 3 times; perform for family. Weak-spot Trainer retake.
Day 14Final lightFlashcards, 'I can' checklist, pack your bag, sleep early.
Only 2 weeks? Do every day. Three weeks? Insert a rest/light day after every 3-4 days and keep the same order.

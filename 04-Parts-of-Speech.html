Module 4: Parts of Speech, Nouns, Pronouns & Articles

Language & Grammar - parts of speech; common, collective and abstract nouns; pronouns; definite and indefinite articles.

Grade 7 · English Home Language (HL) · Term 4 exam revision · 2 x 60 minutes

By the end of this module you can:

  • Name and give examples of the eight parts of speech.
  • Identify common, collective and abstract nouns.
  • Use the different kinds of pronouns correctly.
  • Use the definite and indefinite articles correctly.

1. Learner Notes

The eight parts of speech

Part of speechJobExample
NounNames a person, place, thing or ideateacher, city, courage
PronounReplaces a nounhe, they, mine
VerbShows an action or staterun, is, think
AdjectiveDescribes a nountall, happy, three
AdverbDescribes a verb, adjective or adverbquickly, very, soon
PrepositionShows relationship of place, time or directionunder, before, to
ConjunctionJoins words or clausesand, but, because
InterjectionShows emotionOh!, Wow!

Nouns

TypeMeaningExample
Common nounA general namecity, dog, river
Proper nounA specific name (capital letter)Cape Town, Rover, Thabo
Collective nounA name for a groupa team, a flock, a swarm
Abstract nounAn idea or feeling you cannot see or touchlove, freedom, courage
Concrete nounSomething you can see or touchtable, water, phone

Pronouns

TypeExample
Personal (subject)I, you, he, she, we, they
Personal (object)me, you, him, her, us, them
Possessivemine, yours, his, hers, ours, theirs
Reflexivemyself, yourself, himself, ourselves
Demonstrativethis, that, these, those
Interrogativewho, whom, whose, which, what
Relativewho, which, that
Indefinitesomeone, anybody, everything, none

Articles

ArticleUseExample
aBefore a consonant sound, singular nouna book, a university
anBefore a vowel sound, singular nounan apple, an hour
the (definite)A specific or already-known nounthe book on my desk
  • We use 'the' for things that are unique: the sun, the president.
  • We do not usually use an article before plural or uncountable nouns used generally: 'Cats are friendly.'

4. Worksheet

Section A - Parts of speech

  1. Name the part of speech of each underlined word: She quickly ate the ripe mango.
  2. Write a sentence containing a conjunction and an interjection.

Section B - Nouns

  1. Underline the collective noun: A choir of singers filled the hall.
  2. Classify each noun as common, proper, collective or abstract: Pretoria, honesty, crowd, chair.
  3. Turn the adjective into an abstract noun: kind, brave, free.

Section C - Pronouns

  1. Replace the underlined noun with a pronoun: Thandi and Sue went home.
  2. Choose the correct pronoun: (Who/Whom) is calling?
  3. Supply a reflexive pronoun: He hurt ____ while cycling.

Section D - Articles

  1. Fill in a, an or the: I saw ____ elephant and ____ giraffe at ____ zoo.
  2. Correct the error: She is an honest person and a honour to the team.

7. Remember this!

One-glance recap - if a line feels fuzzy, scroll back to the notes.

  • Noun = naming word. Common (city), collective (team), abstract (a feeling or idea like bravery).
  • Pronoun replaces a noun (he, she, it, they) - make sure it is clear who 'it' is.
  • 'The' = definite (one specific thing). 'A/an' = indefinite (any one thing).
  • 'An' goes before a vowel sound: an apple, an hour.

Lesson plan, differentiation, exit ticket and homework for this module.

Lesson Plan

Learning objectives

  • Name and give examples of the eight parts of speech.
  • Identify common, collective and abstract nouns.
  • Use the different kinds of pronouns correctly.
  • Use the definite and indefinite articles correctly.

Prior knowledge

Learners can recognise nouns and verbs in a sentence.

Resources

Grammar chart, worksheet, whiteboard, dictionary.

Teaching steps

TimeActivity
10 minIntroduce the parts of speech with a labelled sentence.
15 minTeach nouns (common, collective, abstract) and classify examples.
15 minTeach pronouns and their types; guided practice.
10 minTeach articles and the 'a / an / the' rules.
10 minIndependent worksheet practice + exit ticket.

Differentiation, Exit Ticket & Homework

Support

  • Provide a parts-of-speech colour-coding key and sentence strips.

Extension

  • Write a 5-sentence paragraph that includes one of each noun type and three pronoun types.

Exit ticket

Write one collective noun and one abstract noun.

Homework

Find 10 nouns in a magazine article and classify them; write 5 sentences using different pronouns.

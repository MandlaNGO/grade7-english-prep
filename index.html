6. Golden rules for the exam

  • Marks = points. A 2-mark question needs 2 points (or a point + reason).
  • Never leave a blank. An attempt can earn part marks.
  • Read the questions first, then skim and scan the text.
  • Quote evidence from the text in literature and comprehension answers.
  • Own words in the summary - no copied sentences.
  • Format first in transactional writing: wrong format loses marks before content is read.
  • Proofread with C.O.P.S. - Capitals, Order, Punctuation, Spelling.
  • Watch the clock at the halfway point of every paper.
